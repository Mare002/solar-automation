FAQ

Common questions

 

Is the quote binding?

No. The online quote is a calculation based on your answers. The final price is confirmed after the site visit.

Where do I find my monthly consumption?

On your yearly electricity bill: divide the yearly kWh by 12. The assistant can also estimate it from your monthly payment.

Does the orientation of my roof matter?

Yes. A south-facing roof produces the most; east or west produces about 15% less, so the system is sized larger.

Do I need a battery?

Not necessarily. A battery lets you use more of your own electricity in the evening. The quote shows the price with or without it.
